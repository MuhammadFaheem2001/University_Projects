Why K-Nearest Neighbours?

Strengths in this setting

  • Shines on small, low-dimensional datasets — exactly this cohort after MI reduced 94 features to 6.
  • Transparent and easy to interpret: prediction rests on similarity to nearby cases.
  • Minimal parameter tuning required to reach strong performance.

Known limitations

  • High specificity / lower sensitivity — biased toward the majority (cured) class.
  • Suffers from the "curse of dimensionality" if feature count grows.
  • Computationally heavier than tree-based methods at large scale; sensitive to noisy neighbours.
